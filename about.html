About ClickWise

Built around real community interaction.

Our Story

ClickWise began in Fairfax, Virginia, after noticing that many older adults in our community needed help navigating everyday technology. We founded ClickWise to bridge the digital divide by providing free, patient tech support and empowering seniors to stay connected in an increasingly digital world.

Our Mission

ClickWise is a student-led nonprofit that helps older adults navigate technology with confidence. Our teen volunteers provide free tech support and workshops, helping seniors with everything from smartphones and video calls to online safety. We’re passionate about bridging the digital divide and making technology more accessible for the older members of our community.
